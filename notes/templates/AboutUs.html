Welcome to our website!

We have designed and developed a website to make the process of studying and managing educational materials easier and more organized for students. The main goal of this project was to create a practical environment where students can manage their courses, notes, and study materials without confusion and spend more time focusing on learning. In this website, users can categorize their different courses and create separate folders for each subject to store related notes and files. This feature helps students keep their educational materials organized and allows them to quickly find the information they need without wasting time searching through different files and locations.

One of the common problems among students is the disorder and scattering of notes and educational resources. Through this website, we have tried to solve this problem by providing a structured space where all study materials can be stored, categorized, and easily accessed in one place.

The purpose of creating this project was to build a simple and useful tool for students that can reduce the challenges of managing courses and notes. By providing an organized learning environment, we aim to make studying easier, faster, and more effective for students.
